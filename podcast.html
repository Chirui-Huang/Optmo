Podcast Production Workflow

Use this page to collect episode briefs and start the production process.

Workflow Overview

  1. Client submits episode brief and raw files (link to cloud storage).
  2. We perform editing: remove pauses, noise reduction, level and finalize.
  3. Client reviews rough edit and requests revisions.
  4. We deliver final master (WAV + MP3) and show notes.

Deliverables

  • Edited WAV master
  • MP3 for hosting
  • Show notes + timestamps
  • Optional: audiogram / social clip
