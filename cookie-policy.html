Cookie Policy

Effective date: March 6, 2026

This Cookie Policy describes how OPTMO uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function, remember preferences, and provide usage insights.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Category Purpose Can You Disable?
Essential Cookies Required for core site functionality, security, and form/session handling. No. These are necessary for the website to work.
Analytics Cookies Help us understand traffic and improve performance and user experience. Yes. You can decline optional analytics cookies.
Preference Cookies Remember choices such as language or interface preferences. Usually yes, though disabling may reduce convenience.

3. Cookie Consent

When you first visit our site, you may see a cookie notice. You can choose essential-only cookies or accept optional analytics cookies.

4. Managing Cookies

  • You can adjust cookie preferences using our cookie notice options when available.
  • You can also manage cookies in your browser settings.
  • Blocking all cookies may affect website functionality.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party tools integrated into OPTMO may set cookies, such as analytics or embedded services. These cookies are controlled by the respective third parties.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes become effective when posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about our use of cookies, contact optmo2public@gmail.com.
