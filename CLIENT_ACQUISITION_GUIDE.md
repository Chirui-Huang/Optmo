   - Post about OPTMO on your personal profile

## 🔧 Technical Notes

### Contact Form Data
Right now, form submissions are stored in browser localStorage. For production:
1. Set up a backend (I recommend PocketBase - you already have it!)
2. Or use a service like Formspree, Typeform, or Tally
3. Or connect to your email via EmailJS

### Viewing Form Submissions
Open browser console and type:
```javascript
JSON.parse(localStorage.getItem('contact_submissions'))
```
