# Berkeley, CA — Permit & Legal Checklist for Home-based Creative Services

This checklist is targeted at a single-member LLC operating from home in Berkeley, CA offering remote creative services (editing, mixing, video editing, design, digital publishing, ticketing/event promotion). Use it as a starting point — contact the listed agencies to confirm requirements and fees.

## 1. Business formation & tax basics
- Confirm LLC filings with the California Secretary of State (annual/statement of information).
- Obtain an EIN from the IRS (if needed): https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online
- Register for California taxes and sales/use tax if selling taxable goods or services: California Department of Tax and Fee Administration (CDTFA) — https://www.cdtfa.ca.gov

## 2. Local business license & registration
- Check the City of Berkeley Business License requirements and application: https://www.cityofberkeley.info
  - Many cities require a Business License or registration for any business operating in city limits, including home-based businesses.

## 3. Home occupation / zoning
- Contact Berkeley Planning & Development to confirm home-occupation rules for your address (restrictions on client visits, signage, noise, employees, equipment/storage).

## 4. Seller's permit / sales tax
- If you sell physical products or taxable services, register with CDTFA for a seller's permit.

## 5. Event & ticketing permits (if hosting in-person events)
- For in-person events in Berkeley: check with the City of Berkeley Permit Center, Fire Department (for occupancy/standards), and Parks & Rec if using public parks. Permits often required for temporary events, amplified sound, or ticketed gatherings.

## 6. Filming & public-space permits
- If you record/film in public spaces or streets, apply for film/parking/temporary street closure permits via City of Berkeley (or specific parks department).

## 7. Food, alcohol, and health-related permits
- If events include food or alcohol, contact Alameda County Public Health for food vendor requirements and California ABC for alcohol licensing.

## 8. Insurance & liability
- General liability insurance and event insurance policy are strongly recommended for in-person events and vendor work. Ask about certificate of insurance (COI) requirements for venues.

## 9. Music, publishing & IP compliance
- For public performance of music, secure licenses via ASCAP, BMI, SESAC as needed.
- For distribution/publishing of music you do not fully own, secure mechanical and master rights permissions.

## 10. Hiring contractors / payroll
- If you hire contractors or employees, register with CA Employment Development Department (EDD) and follow payroll tax rules; use proper contractor agreements and 1099s/W-2s.

## 11. Contracts, payments & bookkeeping
- Use clear service agreements that define scope, delivery, refunds, licensing rights, and payment terms.
- Set up Stripe/PayPal merchant account and basic bookkeeping (Wave, QuickBooks, or simple spreadsheets).

## 12. Useful links & contacts (starting points)
- City of Berkeley: https://www.cityofberkeley.info
- Berkeley Planning & Development / Permit Center: contact via the City site above
- Alameda County Public Health: https://www.acgov.org
- California Department of Tax and Fee Administration: https://www.cdtfa.ca.gov
- IRS EIN: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online
- ASCAP: https://www.ascap.com • BMI: https://www.bmi.com • SESAC: https://www.sesac.com
